# 📊 Sales & Profit Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales & Profit Dashboard** created using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard is designed to analyze business performance, compare sales and profit, and provide meaningful insights through visualizations and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales and profit across different products.
- Understand country-wise sales contribution.
- Evaluate sales manager performance.
- Compare male and female sales.
- Present business KPIs in a clear and interactive format.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Cost, and Profit.
- **Monthly Sales vs Profit:** Comparison of sales and profit across months.
- **Product-wise Analysis:** Sales and profit comparison across brands/products.
- **Country-wise Sales:** Visualization of sales contribution by country.
- **Sales Manager Performance:** Comparison of sales and profit generated by managers.
- **Male vs Female Analysis:** Comparison of sales by gender.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filters for City, Country, and Sales Manager.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis


## 💡 Key Insights

- Monthly sales and profit can be compared to understand business performance.
- Country-wise analysis helps identify major contributors to overall sales.
- Sales manager analysis provides a view of individual performance.
- Product-wise comparisons help identify products generating higher sales and profit.
- KPI cards provide a quick overview of the business's financial performance.
